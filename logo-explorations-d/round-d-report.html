The question

Does a generated-and-vectorised glyph beat round B’s jawless glyph at 16–48px — and if so, what does it cost in provenance and rebuild work?

Round D exists because that question was worth asking properly rather than assuming. The structural argument for it is real: marks that work at 32px are designed for 32px, whereas the incumbent is a reduction of a drawing made at full size.

The answer

No. The incumbent holds. Twelve generations across two routes, the best three rendered at real pixel sizes against the incumbent on both grounds and both polarities. Nothing generated is recommended for adoption.

Confirming the existing choice with better evidence was a named acceptable outcome of the brief, and it is the outcome. A negative result that closes a question is worth the same as a positive one that opens a door.

THE FIELD

Twelve candidates, two routes

Two generation routes were run: Recraft producing vector directly, and Seedream producing raster to be traced afterwards. Twelve of a budgeted twenty generations were used — stopped early on the brief’s own rule, because both routes had shown their pattern and the marginal generation was buying variance rather than information.

Why the structural argument did not cash

The generated marks are simpler — that part was true. But the simplification consistently spent the detail budget on the generic parts (dome, sockets) and dropped the ape-specific parts. The crest kept becoming a floating gem or a bun — ornament rather than anatomy — in 9 of 12 outputs.

Round B’s glyph was reduced from a drawing that had the anatomy right, and it kept it. That is the whole difference.

This is round 1’s conclusion again, at higher resolution and with a stronger toolchain: generation informs, the band’s own material wins.

PROVENANCE

The exchange was never neutral

Even a winning generated glyph would have carried costs that the incumbent does not. Recorded here because the brief required it, and because it is the part most easily skipped.

  • Trademark-adjacent exposure. The glyph will live on merch, as an avatar, possibly registered. A commercial generation licence is not a warranty of originality against training-data neighbours.
  • A crowded space, confirmed. iStock lists 310+ ape-skull stock illustrations, Dreamstime 154 more, Redbubble has whole ape-skull merch categories, and gorilla-skull logo templates are sold ready-made. A model generating “minimal ape skull glyph” is sampling exactly this space.
What was not checked

One web-search round confirmed the density of the space, and a visual sanity check found no resemblance to the few famous primate band marks. But no reverse-image search was run against any candidate — the tooling was not available. Originality of the generated candidates is therefore unverified, and would have to stay unverified without a proper search.

That is a genuine limitation. It is also now moot, since nothing generated is recommended.

The incumbent’s provenance is not spotless either, and the record is explicit about it: the jawless glyph descends from round B’s generated concepts, unlike the wordmark, which is the band’s own geometry. But it descends from a concept already adjudicated and adopted, its ape anatomy is anchored to the band’s own skull-2013.svg in the redraw spec, and the hand redraw re-authors it. A fresh generated mark restarts that whole question from zero.

On the brief’s own rule — if two candidates are close, the one derived from the band’s own artwork wins on provenance alone — the candidates were not even close.

WHAT THIS CHANGES

One addition, one unblocking

Task 2.2 — unblocked here The “try generating it instead” question is answered and recorded. The remaining blocker is who draws the vectors.
One addition to the redraw Round D confirms at higher confidence which cues survive both generation and reduction. Level sockets and jawlessness survive everywhere; the crest is the fragile, load-bearing cue.

“Crest as a distinct lozenge, clear of the cranium” is the single most important line in the spec. Round D is the second independent session to land on that conclusion. Hold the redraw to it.

HONEST LIMITS

What this round cannot tell you

  • Two Illustrator vectorisation routes were not run — the Adobe integration was unauthorised in the session. ILLUSTRATOR-STEPS.md holds the manual procedure. Their absence cannot change the verdict: traced output does not ship regardless, and those routes only compete on sketch fidelity.

  • No reverse-image search on any candidate. See provenance above.
